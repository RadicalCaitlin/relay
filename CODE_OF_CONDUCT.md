# Code of Conduct

Relay follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where the
project is represented.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Accept that people have different experience, backgrounds and ways of working.
- Give and take feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or attention, or publishing someone's private
  information.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant is the authority where this summary is silent.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately, using the email address
on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/RadicalCaitlin). Do not raise it in a
public issue.

Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer will review each one and may respond with a
correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project's spaces, in line with
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
